Swieom Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · Android and iPhone app "Swieom" (com.gwanggo.swieom) 1.0.0 · 한국어

Summary — Swieom needs no sign-up and the developer runs no server. Your focus and break history, moods, tasks, points, wardrobe and settings are stored only on your phone. Payments are handled by Google Play (Android) and the App Store (iPhone).

3. What leaves your phone

Nothing is sent to the developer. The app uses no advertising or analytics SDKs.
· Payments: point packs and the "Swieom Plus" subscription are processed by Google Play or the App Store under their own policies. The app only receives whether a purchase succeeded.
· Backup: when you tap "Save to file", a backup file is written to the place you choose. The app does not send it anywhere else.
· Notifications, lock screen, widgets and smartwatches show information on your own devices; smartwatches mirror phone notifications.

4. Retention and deletion

Deleting the app deletes all records on the phone. Backup files you saved can be deleted by you.

5. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with its effective date.
