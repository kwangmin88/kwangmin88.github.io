Korean Saju Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · Android app 'Korean Saju: Eastern Astrology' (com.gwanggo.fourpillars) 0.1.4

Summary — The birth details you enter (date, time, birthplace, optional name) are used only to calculate your chart on this phone and are saved only on this phone. They are not sent to the developer's servers or to any other company. The app has no accounts and this version shows no ads. The optional 'full reading' is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Google Play.

3. Information that leaves your phone

Only when you tap 'Share my chart card' is a picture of your chart (pillars and personality title, without your name) passed to the app you choose. Otherwise the app does not send your birth details anywhere. Calculations, including time zones and the Vietnamese lunar calendar, are done on the phone.

4. Payments

The full reading is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Payment details such as card numbers are handled only by Google Play; neither the app nor the developer receives them. The app receives from Google Play only whether this Google account owns the product, to unlock the reading. You can request a refund through Google Play. Refunded purchases lock the reading again.

5. Ads, accounts and third parties

This version contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no login. We do not share information with or entrust its processing to any third party other than Google Play for payments (section 4). If a future version adds ads, analytics or accounts, we will update this page first and change the effective date.
