Next Visit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Android app "Next Visit" (package com.gwanggo.dangol)

Summary — The customer notes you type into the app, and what the app reads from your phone calls and message notifications, are stored only on your phone. Three things can leave your phone: (1) call-recording audio, only if you turn on "Find appointments in calls" and choose your call-recording folder — it is turned into text to find appointments and is not stored; (2) anonymous usage statistics (your business type and how often features are used — no customer information; you can turn this off in Settings); and (3) a Google Play purchase token and a random install number, if you buy a plan or extra call analyses, so our server can confirm the purchase with Google. We use no advertising tools and no third-party analytics tools (such as Google Analytics).

3. Recording calls — your responsibility

The app never records calls itself. The optional call finder reads recordings made by your phone's own call-recorder. Laws about recording phone calls differ by country and state: in many places you must tell the other person and get their consent first. You are responsible for following the law that applies to you and for getting any consent that is required before you record a call and before you let the app analyse it. Recording is also unavailable on some phones, carriers and countries; without it the app still works as your customer notebook, calendar and morning reminder.

4. Your customers' personal data

Customer names and numbers are information you put into the app for your business. We cannot see them. You are the controller of your customers' data: you decide the purpose, you tell your customers and, where the law requires, obtain their consent. The app never sends marketing messages by itself; "Send" opens your messaging app with the text filled in and you press send.

5. Processors, international transfers and legal bases

We share no information with third parties for their own purposes. When the call finder is on, we use Cloudflare, Inc. (server hosting and AI speech-to-text and text-analysis, in Cloudflare's data centers worldwide, including the United States) as our processor; it may process the audio and text only to give us that result. Anonymous statistics and usage counts are stored in the same provider's database. Purchases are confirmed with Google LLC (Google Play). Our company is in Korea, so the data our server handles is processed in Korea and in the provider's data centers outside your country; we rely on our providers' data-processing terms for those transfers. Where the GDPR / UK GDPR applies, our legal bases are: your consent (call finder, notification access, calendar link, usage statistics — you can withdraw it any time by turning the feature off), performance of a contract (confirming your purchase and applying your plan) and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and keeping the service running (daily limits). We do not make decisions with legal effects about you by automated means; the call finder only suggests requests that you confirm.

6. Retention and deleting your data

On your phone, records stay until you delete them or uninstall the app. One customer: "Delete customer" at the bottom of their page. Everything: "Delete all records" at the bottom of Settings. Uninstalling removes everything stored on the phone. Permissions can be switched off in your phone's settings at any time; customers, visits and bookings can still be entered by hand. On our server there is no audio, transcript, customer or message data to delete; for the random install number, usage counts and purchase records, write to us at the address below and, if you can, tell us roughly when you installed (the number is random, so we can only find it with the install's own token — if you uninstall, we remove the usage records after one year of inactivity).

8. Children

This app is for people who run a business and is not aimed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

9. Security

Data sent to our server travels over HTTPS. The server keeps no audio or transcripts. The app's storage is private to the app on your phone; protect your phone with a screen lock.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with its date. If a new feature sends information off the phone (for example cloud backup), the app will tell you first and ask for your consent.
