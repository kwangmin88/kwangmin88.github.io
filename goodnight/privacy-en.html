Sweet Sleep Lab Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · Android and iPhone app 'Sweet Sleep Lab (꿀잠 연구소)' (com.gwanggo.sleep) 1.1.0

Summary — With your permission, Sweet Sleep Lab only reads your sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability and step records from Health Connect (on iPhone, from the Health app via HealthKit). The health records it reads are used only to show and calculate results on this phone. They are not sent to the developer's servers or to any other company. Since 1.1.0 the app uses the internet: on Android, while you use the app for free, a Google AdMob banner ad appears at the bottom of the screen, and Pro (paid) purchases are handled by Google Play. Health records are not used for ads or payments. The iPhone app shows no ads, and Pro purchases are handled by the App Store.

3. How we use Health Connect data

Information received from Health Connect is used only to show you the features above. It is never used for advertising, sold, used for credit or insurance assessments, or passed on to anyone else. No person reads that data.
The use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
Information received from the Health app (HealthKit) on iPhone is handled under the same principles and is never used for advertising or sold.

4. Information that leaves your phone

Only when you tap 'Share report' (Android) is a text summary of your sleep (time slept, score, time in each stage, suggested bedtime) passed to the chat or app you choose. Otherwise, the app does not send information off your phone.

5. Ads (while using the app for free)

While you are not using Pro, the app shows an AdMob banner ad from Google LLC at the bottom of the screen. To show ads and prevent invalid clicks, the AdMob SDK sends your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) and records of ads viewed and tapped to Google. Health records (sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, steps) are never used for ads or passed to AdMob. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages (rated PG or lower).
You can delete your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads). Google's processing of information follows Google's partner sites policy. No ads are shown while you use Pro. This section applies only to the Android app; the iPhone app shows no ads.

6. Payments (Pro)

Pro subscriptions (monthly or yearly) and the one-time (lifetime) purchase are handled by Google Play. Payment details such as card numbers are handled only by Google Play; neither the app nor the developer receives them. The app receives from Google Play only whether this account has bought Pro (purchase status, product and purchase token) to unlock Pro features, and remembers the result on this phone. You can cancel or request a refund in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
On iPhone, Pro is handled by the App Store. The app receives only the purchase status from the App Store, and you can cancel in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

7. Sharing with third parties, processors and retention

Apart from sections 5 and 6 above (Google LLC — ad display and Google Play payments / Apple — App Store payments), we do not share information with or entrust its processing to any third party. App settings are deleted when you delete the app. Health Connect records are managed by Health Connect and your watch maker's app (e.g. Samsung Health), and you can turn off this app's read access or delete records at any time in your phone's Settings → Health Connect. On iPhone, you can turn off this app's read access in the Health app → your profile → Apps.

9. Not for medical use

Sleep stages are shown exactly as your watch maker scores them; naps, stress and alertness are estimates calculated from heart rate. The app is not a medical device for diagnosing, treating or preventing any disease.

10. Children

11. Changes
